### Can I join an Abyss boat dive?

Yes, if you are a certified diver and the **particular departure** fits your training, recent experience and confidence. Open Water is the general minimum. A dive **deeper than 18 m requires Advanced Open Water or an equivalent qualification**; technical dives require the qualification and experience shown in the listing. Abyss's local booking rules also say that a diver with **fewer than 20 logged dives must have dived within the previous 12 months**. Bring proof of certification and tell the team if you have been out of the water or have concerns about your skills.

### Which departure should I choose?

Start with your certification and maximum trained depth, then check the **specific event's** depth range, experience requirement, conditions and planned site. An Open Water diver can choose a suitable recreational reef or shallow wreck listing within their limit; an experienced Advanced diver can consider deeper reefs and walls when the listing and recent experience fit. Technical wrecks require a separate, appropriate qualification and plan. If you feel rusty, [a scuba refresher](https://www.abyss.com.au/scuba-refresher-course) or a suitable guided shore dive may help you return comfortably.

Do **not** classify a date solely by its site name. Henry Head is often described as an 8–12 m dive, but one current Henry Head departure is advertised at **12–24 m**. That event's stated maximum is beyond Open Water limits. Magic Point likewise has profiles that vary by departure; a shark-dive name alone does not establish suitability. Abyss can help match an individual diver to a listing after learning their qualification, logged dives, last dive and comfortable depth.

### What might I see?

Depending on the site, Sydney boat dives can reach sponge gardens, reefs, walls, Grey Nurse Shark habitat and wrecks. Magic Point's sharks and all other wildlife are wild, so sightings cannot be guaranteed. The [Sydney boat-charter page](https://www.abyss.com.au/sydney-boat-diving-charters) shows the current choices; the [Sydney Dive Calendar](https://www.abyss.com.au/sydney-dive-calendar), dive-site guides and marine-life knowledge give additional detail. Choose a date that suits your qualification, then ask the Abyss team if its individual suitability is unclear.

### Where and when do we meet?

Most local Sydney departures use **Fisherman's Wharf at Sans Souci Park**, about five minutes from the Abyss shop, with free all-day parking near the boat. Some wildlife and specialist charters leave elsewhere. Your **individual listing and booking confirmation** give the final meeting point and time. Plan to arrive **30–45 minutes before departure unless your confirmation says otherwise**, so you can check in, prepare gear and hear the briefing. The boat cannot normally wait for late arrivals. Trip duration varies with the destination, sea state, dive plan and surface interval; allow flexibility after the advertised finish.

### Is there a toilet or shower?

**Yes.** Abyss describes its 12.5 m Sydney dive boat as having an **onboard toilet and two hot deck showers**, plus dry undercover seating and an open deck for gearing up. The showers are on the deck; do not assume a private enclosed shower or changing room. Bring a towel and warm clothes for after diving. If a different vessel or special departure is used, confirm its facilities with the team.

### What is the entry and exit like?

The boat has a fold-down waterline platform and two ladders for returning aboard. The crew briefs divers on the day's entry, exit, buddy and emergency procedures. Follow their directions and ask for help before entering if you are worried about mobility or lifting gear. A non-diving passenger place, accessibility arrangement or other individual need requires confirmation before booking.

### How many dives and tanks should I plan for?

**Most daytime recreational outings are double dives**, so most divers need **two tanks**. Night, technical, wildlife and other specialist departures may differ. Bring complete, serviceable gear for the planned dive, or add the rental items you need while booking. Tanks, weights and equipment are **not automatically included**: inspect the specific listing and the booking selections before checkout. Arrange hire in advance, rather than assuming gear is available at the wharf.

Each diver needs a **dive computer, surface marker buoy (SMB) and audible signalling device**, along with the rest of the equipment required by the departure. Bring certification details or an eCard, water, sun protection, a towel and warm clothes. Ask about any gas, Nitrox or specialist equipment needed for your selected event rather than assuming it is supplied.

### Can I come without a buddy? Will someone guide me underwater?

**You can book alone.** The Divemaster can help arrange a suitable buddy team based on the divers and the planned dive. The trip includes a skipper, Divemaster and site and safety briefing, but **in-water guiding varies by departure**. If you need an instructor, private guide or continuous underwater escort, check the exact listing and ask Abyss before paying. A buddy pairing does not itself promise an in-water guide.

### How can I reduce the chance of seasickness?

Prepare and check your gear before the boat reaches open water, so you spend less time looking down. If you are prone to motion sickness, eat a **light meal**, drink water, look toward the horizon and choose a stable, well-ventilated position near the centre of the boat when the crew permits. Avoid prolonged reading or looking at your phone, and let the crew know early if you feel unwell. These measures help some people but cannot guarantee that you will feel well.

Ask a **pharmacist or doctor before the trip** about any seasickness medicine or patch and whether it is suitable for diving with your circumstances. Some cause drowsiness, blurred vision or other effects that can impair diving. Do not first try a medicine on dive day. If you are vomiting, dizzy, dehydrated or feel unable to dive safely, tell the crew and do not assume getting into the water will solve it. The crew and a medical professional can advise on the immediate situation; the Beacon cannot medically clear you to dive.

### What makes the day run smoothly for everyone?

Arrive early, bring the gear and tanks required for your booking, keep belongings compact, secure loose items and set up when the crew directs. Listen to the full boat and site briefing, agree on the buddy and return plan, and be ready when it is your turn to enter or reboard. Follow crew instructions around the platform, ladders, tanks and deck space. Questions about a particular day's conditions or site should go to the crew before entry.

For more preparation, read [Abyss's motion-sickness tips](https://www.abyss.com.au/blog/dive-tips/tips-for-motion-sickness) and [dive-boat etiquette guide](https://www.abyss.com.au/blog/boat-diving/dive-boat-etiquette-). The medicine guidance above follows [Divers Alert Network's seasickness advice](https://dan.org/safety-prevention/diver-safety/divers-blog/seasickness-prevention-and-treatment/).

### Can weather change the dive?

Yes. Wind, swell, current, visibility and safety can change the scheduled site or plan. The **skipper makes the final departure and site decision**; a forecast or a Beacon answer cannot confirm it. Check the dated [dive-conditions report](https://www.abyss.com.au/dive-conditions), then follow the latest instructions for your own departure. A different suitable site is possible, and wildlife is never guaranteed.

### What if I cancel or need to change dates?

The current [boat-dive booking terms](https://www.abyss.com.au/charters/boat-dives) say that cancellation **more than three days** before the dive is refundable less a **A$25 administration fee**. **Within three days**, there is no refund. A late arrival, missed boat or no-show has no refund or credit and requires a full-cost rebooking. One date transfer requested more than three days ahead is free; later or additional date changes carry a **A$100 rebooking fee**. A transfer to another suitably qualified diver also carries A$100. If you choose not to complete a scheduled dive, the unused dive is forfeited. The same cancellation timing applies if a medical or fitness issue prevents participation.

If **Abyss cancels for weather or safety**, the published terms allow transfer to another available date **within 12 months**, with any price difference at rebooking. Rental gear has its **own** refund windows: more than 48 hours before pickup, refund less 20% of hire (minimum A$10); 48–24 hours, 50% retained; under 24 hours or no-show, 100% retained. Check the current terms before booking and contact Abyss promptly for a booking-specific change or dispute. The Beacon can explain published terms but cannot grant an exception or confirm a refund.

### Can I book a technical wreck dive?

Only when your **technical qualification, experience, equipment and planned gases** match that **specific dive's** depth and requirements, and Abyss confirms your suitability. The [technical boat-dives page](https://www.abyss.com.au/charters/tech-boat-dives) describes the **SS Tuggerah at about 46 m** and **SS Kelloe at about 48 m**. These depths exceed recreational limits; a recreational Deep or Tec 40 certification alone should not be represented as qualifying a diver for a 46–48 m wreck. The current Tuggerah listing explicitly says the diver must be technically certified.

Read the live technical event before booking and give the team your exact qualification, logged dives to comparable depth, recent experience, equipment configuration and gas plan. The broad tech-page wording about proof of qualification **or** logged depth is ambiguous; logged dives alone do not replace the qualification required by an individual event. The team must resolve any mismatch. Tech departures can differ from the usual two-dive recreational format, meeting point, tanks and equipment inclusions. Check their current listing and confirmation.

If you are not qualified for a deep technical profile, choose a [recreational or advanced boat dive](https://www.abyss.com.au/sydney-boat-diving-charters) within your limits or ask Abyss about a training pathway.
